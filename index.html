The follow-ups you're chasing, chased for you.

FollowOps is a Chrome extension for people who run properties and operations. Capture a follow-up from any page in seconds, and keep nudging it — snooze, reschedule, log attempts — until it's actually done.

Local-first · No account · No cloud · No tracking

Built for the chase

Capture from anywhere

Select text on any page, right-click "Add to FollowOps", or hit Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+F. The source page and quoted text ride along with the follow-up.

A Today view that writes itself

Overdue, due today, and upcoming — grouped, prioritized, and ready to work through every morning.

One-click "Followed Up"

Log the attempt and immediately schedule the next nudge. Nothing goes quiet just because someone didn't reply.

Contacts & properties

Every follow-up is tied to a person and a place, with per-property categories like Plumbing, Insurance, or Inspection.

Checklists & history

Action items, notes, and a full activity trail on every follow-up — you always know what happened and when.

Reminders, if you want them

Notifications are optional and off by default. Enable them and Chrome nudges you when a follow-up comes due.

Private by design

Everything lives in your browser's local database. There is no backend, no account, no sync, and no analytics — FollowOps never sends your data anywhere. Export it as JSON whenever you like; it's yours.
